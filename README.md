📊 BlinkIt Power BI Dashboard – Grocery Sales Analytics

Welcome to the BlinkIt Grocery Sales Dashboard, a dynamic Power BI report built to analyze sales, consumer preferences, and outlet performance across India. The dashboard leverages DAX measures, visual storytelling, and parameter-based analysis to provide insights that drive smarter retail decisions.

🧠 Project Objective

This dashboard empowers stakeholders to:

Monitor total and average sales performance

Understand customer preferences by fat content and item type

Analyze outlet impact by location, size, and type

Drive inventory and marketing strategy through data

📦 Dataset Summary

The dataset consists of historical sales and operations data from BlinkIt (India’s rapid delivery platform). It includes:

Product attributes (type, fat content, item weight)

Outlet features (size, tier, type, establishment year)

Sales, ratings, and item visibility data

📐 DAX Measures Used
Measure	DAX Code

Total Sales	Total Sales = SUM('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Sales])

Average Sales	Avg Sales = AVERAGE('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Sales])

Number of Items	No of Items = DISTINCTCOUNT('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Item Identifier])

Average Rating	Avg Rating = AVERAGE('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Rating])

These measures are bundled into a "Metrics" parameter for dynamic filtering in the FAT CONTENT visual.

🍶 FAT CONTENT (Donut Chart)

Purpose: Visualizes the contribution of Low Fat vs Regular products to selected metric.

Dynamic Control: Controlled by Metrics parameter (Total Sales, Avg Sales, No. of Items, Avg Rating).

Insight:

Low Fat items lead in total sales with $776.32K (~65%), compared to $425.36K (~35%) from Regular fat products.

This indicates a growing consumer shift toward health-conscious choices — an opportunity for product line expansion and premium pricing.

🥫 ITEM TYPE (Bar Chart)

Purpose: Displays total sales by item category.

Top 3 Categories:

Fruits and Vegetables – ~$0.18M
Snack Foods – ~$0.18M
Household Items – ~$0.14M

Insight: These are your key sales drivers. Seasonal promotion bundles and priority placement can enhance revenue from these categories.

📅 OUTLET ESTABLISHMENT (Line Chart)

Purpose: Examines how outlet launch year impacts sales.

Insight:

Highest sales generated by outlets established in 2018 (~$205K). Moderate recovery observed in 2022 after a dip.

Reviewing successful 2018 strategies may guide new outlet launches.

🏪 OUTLET SIZE (Donut Chart)
Purpose: Visual breakdown of total sales by outlet size (Small, Medium, High).

Insight:

Medium-sized outlets dominate with over $507.90K in sales, suggesting they strike the best balance between scale and efficiency.

🌐 OUTLET LOCATION (Bar Chart)

Purpose: Shows total sales across urban tiers (Tier 1, 2, 3).

Insight:

Tier 3 cities lead with $472.13K in sales — a strong indicator of opportunity in less urban markets.

🧁 FAT BY OUTLET (Bar Chart)

Purpose: Compares fat content preference across different outlet locations.

Insight:

Low Fat products dominate across all outlet types, reinforcing the health-conscious buying trend.

📋 OUTLET TYPE (Matrix Table)

Fields Included:

Total Sales
Number of Items
Avg Sales
Avg Rating
Item Visibility
Insight:

Supermarket Type 1 leads with $787.55K in sales and 5577 unique items.

Grocery Stores have high item visibility (0.10), though their total sales are lower (~$38.4K).

🚀 Growth Strategies

Based on the dashboard’s insights:

1. Expand in Tier 3 Cities
Strong sales potential in less urban regions.
Scale operations, partner with local vendors, and offer hyperlocal promotions.

3. Invest in Medium-Sized Outlets
Highest revenue-contributing size segment.
Optimize for stock efficiency and fast delivery models.

5. Focus on Fruits, Snack Foods, and Household Essentials
These categories drive the highest sales volume.
Introduce bundle deals, subscription boxes, and seasonal packs to increase cart size.

7. Enhance Visibility in Low-Performing Outlets
Supermarket Type 2 and Type 3 outlets show lower visibility and engagement.
Improve in-store layouts and app-based product placement algorithms.

9. Double Down on Low Fat Products
Clearly dominant in sales.
Consider launching sub-brands focused on health-conscious SKUs and include nutrition info highlights.

11. Optimize Outlet Launch Strategy
Review 2018 playbook for successful rollouts.
Match launch tactics with high-performing regions and outlet types.
